# Challenge M4–M6–01: Escaping Ocean Painting

## Concept

Inside a contemporary art gallery, a huge painted ocean wave breaks through its
frame. Water flows across the gallery floor and a small boat escapes from the
painting. A squid curator in a suit stands on a ladder and uses a paint roller
to push the wave back into the artwork.

The visual joke is a painting refusing to stay flat and an art curator trying to
repair reality.

## Core scoring anchors

- Ocean wave inside a painting
- Wave breaking out through the frame
- Water entering the gallery
- Squid curator trying to push it back

## Optional details

- Small boat
- Ladder
- Paint roller
- Contemporary gallery
- Dramatic exhibition lighting

## Example short prompt

> คลื่นทะเลในภาพวาดทะลุกรอบออกมาท่วมแกลเลอรี โดยมีปลาหมึกภัณฑารักษ์พยายามดันกลับเข้าไป

## Evaluation notes

This challenge expects a layered relationship between artwork, physical space, and
character action. The transformation and composition matter more than every
museum prop or water detail.

## Feedback focus

Praise recognition of the boundary-breaking artwork and the curator’s response.
Suggest one composition, atmosphere, or secondary detail when the main concept
is already clear.
