# Challenge P1–P3–02: Turtle Driving a Flying Bus

## Concept

A cheerful green turtle wearing a blue driver’s cap drives a bright yellow bus
floating through the sky. Three friendly butterflies with tiny backpacks are
passengers, and one presses the bus bell.

The visual joke is a turtle driving a flying bus while butterflies ride inside.

## Core scoring anchors

- Turtle driving a bus
- Yellow bus flying in the sky
- Butterflies as passengers

## Optional details

- Blue driver’s cap
- Tiny backpacks
- Bus bell
- Rainbow
- Friendly clouds and the bus’s playful wings

## Example short prompt

> เต่าขับรถบัสสีเหลืองพาผีเสื้อบินอยู่บนท้องฟ้า

## Evaluation notes

A concise prompt can capture the core joke. Do not require the player to name
every butterfly, cloud, or decoration. The subject, action, and unusual setting
matter most for the youngest level.

## Feedback focus

Praise a clear description of who is driving, what is flying, and who is riding.
Offer optional details as a way to make the scene more specific.
