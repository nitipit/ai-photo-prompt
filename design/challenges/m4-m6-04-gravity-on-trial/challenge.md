# Challenge M4–M6–04: Gravity on Trial

## Concept

Inside a courtroom floating in the sky, a dark glowing sphere represents gravity
and sits in the defendant’s box. Chairs, shoes, papers, and a pendulum float away
from the floor. An owl judge strikes the gavel while an animal lawyer points at
the floating evidence.

The visual joke is gravity being treated as a defendant in a legal case because
everything has started floating.

## Core scoring anchors

- Courtroom setting
- Gravity represented as the defendant
- Objects floating around the courtroom
- Judge conducting the trial

## Optional details

- Gavel
- Animal lawyer
- Floating papers
- Pendulum
- Courtroom suspended in the sky

## Example short prompt

> แรงโน้มถ่วงถูกฟ้องในศาล เพราะทำให้สิ่งของทุกอย่างลอยขึ้นจากพื้น

## Evaluation notes

This challenge introduces a more conceptual visual joke. The courtroom, gravity
as a defendant, floating evidence, and judge action carry the scene; the player
does not need to describe every legal prop.

## Feedback focus

Praise recognition of the metaphor and the visible consequence. Suggest one
specific floating object or courtroom detail when the main concept is already
clear.
