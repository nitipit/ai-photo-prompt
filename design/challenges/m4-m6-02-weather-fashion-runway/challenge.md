# Challenge M4–M6–02: Weather Fashion Runway

## Concept

On a floating runway in the sky, a flamingo model walks in a dress made from
sunset colors, clouds, and golden light. A small crow designer sews lightning
into the hem while animal guests watch from cloud seats.

The visual joke is a fashion show where weather becomes clothing.

## Core scoring anchors

- Flamingo model walking on a sky runway
- Dress made from sunset and clouds
- Crow designer sewing lightning
- Fashion show in the sky

## Optional details

- Golden light
- Cloud audience
- Sewing tools
- Glowing runway
- Evening sky colors

## Example short prompt

> ฟลามิงโกเดินแบบบนรันเวย์ลอยฟ้าใส่ชุดที่ทำจากพระอาทิตย์ตกและสายฟ้า

## Evaluation notes

This challenge rewards a layered visual concept involving fashion, weather, and
character roles. The model, weather-based clothing, designer action, and sky
runway matter more than every audience or lighting detail.

## Feedback focus

Praise recognition of the unusual material and the designer’s action. Suggest one
styling or composition detail when the central concept is already clear.
