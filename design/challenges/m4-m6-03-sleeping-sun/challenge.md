# Challenge M4–M6–03: Sleeping Sun

## Concept

Above a city that is still dark, the sun sleeps on a cloud bed and tries to hit
snooze on a giant alarm clock. A stylish owl clockmaker drags the alarm closer
to wake it, while morning light leaks from beneath the sun’s blanket.

The visual joke is a sun treating sunrise like a late morning alarm.

## Core scoring anchors

- Sun sleeping on a cloud bed
- Sun pressing snooze on an alarm clock
- Owl clockmaker trying to wake the sun
- Dark city waiting for morning

## Optional details

- Sun pajamas or blanket
- Oversized alarm clock
- Owl’s suit and glasses
- Light leaking from the blanket
- Dark city at dawn

## Example short prompt

> พระอาทิตย์นอนหลับและกดเลื่อนปลุก ขณะที่นกฮูกพยายามปลุกให้เมืองสว่าง

## Evaluation notes

This challenge rewards visual personification and a clear cause-and-effect scene.
The sleeping sun, alarm action, owl’s role, and waiting city matter more than
every costume or dawn detail.

## Feedback focus

Praise recognition of the unusual situation and the relationship between the sun,
alarm, and city. Suggest one atmosphere or character detail when the main idea
is already clear.
