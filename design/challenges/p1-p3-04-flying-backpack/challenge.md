# Challenge P1–P3–04: School Backpack with Wings

## Concept

A friendly red school backpack has colorful butterfly wings and flies above a
sunny playground. Books and pencils peek out of the backpack as it travels
through the sky.

The visual joke is an ordinary school object transforming into a flying
creature.

## Core scoring anchors

- Red school backpack
- Butterfly wings
- Backpack flying above a playground or in the sky

## Optional details

- Books and pencils
- Friendly face
- Playground equipment
- Clouds
- Bright sunlight

## Example short prompt

> กระเป๋านักเรียนสีแดงมีปีกบินอยู่บนท้องฟ้า

## Evaluation notes

The transformation and flight are more important than the background. A short
prompt can score strongly without naming the books, pencils, or playground
objects.

## Feedback focus

Praise the player for identifying the ordinary object and its surprising new
ability. Suggest one color or setting detail as an optional improvement.
