# Challenge P1–P3–05: Panda on a Banana Rocket

## Concept

A cute baby panda wearing a pilot helmet rides a rocket shaped like a yellow
banana through a purple-blue sky toward a friendly smiling moon.

The visual joke is an impossible mashup of a panda, a banana, and a rocket.

## Core scoring anchors

- Baby panda
- Rocket shaped like a banana
- Flying toward the moon

## Optional details

- Pilot helmet
- Colorful stars
- Smiling moon
- Soft clouds
- Small flame trail

## Example short prompt

> แพนด้านั่งจรวดกล้วยบินไปดวงจันทร์

## Evaluation notes

The three-part mashup is enough for a strong short prompt. Stars, clouds, and
the helmet should be treated as optional richness rather than requirements.

## Feedback focus

Praise the player for identifying the unusual combination and direction of
movement. Offer one optional detail to make the image more specific.
