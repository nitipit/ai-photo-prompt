# Challenge M1–M3–05: Weather DJ

## Concept

On a city rooftop at night, a fox DJ wearing headphones plays music on a
turntable. Sound waves from the speakers rise into a storm and transform the
dark clouds into colorful dancing clouds. Lightning becomes neon ribbons and
raindrops become confetti.

The visual joke is music changing a storm into a dance party.

## Core scoring anchors

- Fox DJ
- Music equipment and speakers
- Storm transforming into a colorful dance party
- City rooftop at night

## Optional details

- Headphones
- Turntable
- Neon ribbons
- Confetti rain
- Colorful clouds or animal audience

## Example short prompt

> จิ้งจอกดีเจเปิดเพลงบนดาดฟ้าแล้วเปลี่ยนพายุให้เป็นงานเต้นรำสีสันสดใส

## Evaluation notes

This challenge rewards a clear cause-and-effect relationship between the music
and the changing weather. The DJ, storm, transformation, and rooftop setting
matter more than every sound-wave or party detail.

## Feedback focus

Praise recognition of what causes the transformation and what the storm becomes.
Suggest one music, weather, or atmosphere detail when the main idea is already
clear.
