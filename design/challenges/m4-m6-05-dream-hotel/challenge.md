# Challenge M4–M6–05: Dream Hotel

## Concept

Inside a luxurious hotel lobby at night, a robot concierge stores dreams in
glass rooms and bottles. One dream escapes as a giant glowing flying fish,
swimming through the lobby ceiling while small guests look up in surprise.

The visual joke is a hotel that checks in dreams and accidentally lets one
escape.

## Core scoring anchors

- Night-time hotel lobby
- Robot concierge storing dreams
- Dreams contained in glass rooms or bottles
- Giant glowing flying fish escaping

## Optional details

- Reception desk
- Room keys
- Small guests
- Glowing light
- Glass rooms containing different dreams

## Example short prompt

> โรงแรมกลางคืนรับฝากความฝันไว้ในขวดแก้ว แล้วมีปลาบินยักษ์หลุดออกมา

## Evaluation notes

This challenge closes the level with a layered narrative scene. The hotel,
dream-storage action, escaping dream, and flying fish are more important than
every guest, key, or glass-room detail.

## Feedback focus

Praise recognition of the unusual service and the escaped dream. Suggest one
story, atmosphere, or composition detail when the central scene is already clear.
