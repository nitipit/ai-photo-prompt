# Challenge P1–P3–01: Rabbit Chef and Giant Pancake

## Concept

A huge fluffy white rabbit wearing a red chef hat flips an enormous pancake
shaped like a smiling sun in a bright kitchen. A tiny blue mouse climbs a
ladder and sprinkles colorful sugar stars.

The visual joke is a giant rabbit and a tiny mouse working together in an
oversized kitchen.

## Core scoring anchors

- Giant white rabbit as a chef
- Making or flipping a giant pancake
- Tiny mouse and a clear size contrast

## Optional details

- Red chef hat
- Wooden ladder
- Colorful sugar stars
- Turquoise pan
- Warm kitchen light and strawberries

## Example short prompt

> กระต่ายตัวใหญ่ทำแพนเค้กกับหนูตัวเล็ก

## Evaluation notes

For ป.1–ป.3, clarity matters more than length. A short prompt can be strong
when it captures the main scene and relationship. Optional details provide
precision but are not required for a strong result.

## Feedback focus

Praise recognition of the characters, the main action, and the size
relationship. Suggest one extra visual detail only when useful.
