# Challenge M1–M3–01: Escaping Shadow

## Concept

In a rainy neon city, an orange cat wearing a raincoat chases its own giant
shadow. The shadow has detached from the ground and is skateboarding away down
the wet street. Reflections, tall buildings, and cinematic light make the scene
feel like a playful adventure film.

The visual joke is a shadow that has become independent and is escaping its
owner.

## Core scoring anchors

- Orange cat
- Giant shadow detached from the cat
- Shadow escaping or skateboarding
- Rainy neon city

## Optional details

- Raincoat
- Wet reflective street
- Tall buildings
- Abstract sign lights without text
- Comedic chase pose

## Example short prompt

> แมวไล่ตามเงายักษ์ของตัวเองที่หนีไปบนถนนเมืองนีออน

## Evaluation notes

This challenge introduces a more surreal relationship and cinematic setting.
The detached shadow, escape action, and city atmosphere matter more than every
reflection or background light.

## Feedback focus

Praise recognition of the unusual relationship between the cat and its shadow.
Suggest one action or atmosphere detail when the central concept is already
clear.
