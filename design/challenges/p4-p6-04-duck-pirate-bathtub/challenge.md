# Challenge P4–P6–04: Duck Pirate Bathtub

## Concept

A yellow duck wearing a pirate hat captains a blue bathtub that works as a
boat on a river of chocolate. Small duck crew members paddle and hold a map.
Cookie islands, star-shaped clouds, and a floating treasure chest surround the
journey.

The visual joke is a duck pirate adventure using a bathtub as a boat on a
chocolate river.

## Core scoring anchors

- Duck pirate captain
- Bathtub functioning as a boat
- Sailing on a chocolate river
- Treasure journey with a crew or treasure chest

## Optional details

- Pirate hat
- Map and paddles
- Cookie islands
- Star-shaped clouds
- Floating treasure chest

## Example short prompt

> เป็ดโจรสลัดขับเรืออ่างอาบน้ำล่องบนแม่น้ำช็อกโกแลตไปหาสมบัติ

## Evaluation notes

This challenge rewards a clear setting and adventure relationship. The player
does not need to name every crew member, cookie island, or cloud. The unusual
boat and chocolate river are the strongest visual anchors.

## Feedback focus

Praise the player for identifying the captain, vehicle, and unusual environment.
Suggest one adventure detail when the main scene is already clear.
