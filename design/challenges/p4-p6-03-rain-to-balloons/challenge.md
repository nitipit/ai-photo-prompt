# Challenge P4–P6–03: Rain-to-Balloon Factory

## Concept

On a rooftop, three squirrel inventors wearing goggles operate a colorful machine.
The machine receives raindrops from a purple rain cloud through a large funnel
and transforms them into balloons. One squirrel turns a crank, one catches the
balloons, and one points excitedly at the result.

The visual joke is an invention that changes ordinary rain into colorful
balloons.

## Core scoring anchors

- Squirrel inventors controlling a machine
- Machine receiving raindrops
- Raindrops transforming into balloons
- Cloud producing the rain

## Optional details

- Inventor goggles
- Crank, gears, and pipes
- Rooftop
- City in the distance
- Rainbow and balloons of different colors

## Example short prompt

> กระรอกนักประดิษฐ์ใช้เครื่องเปลี่ยนหยดฝนจากก้อนเมฆให้เป็นลูกโป่งสี ๆ

## Evaluation notes

This challenge can reward a clear transformation and the relationships between
characters, machine, cloud, and output. Players do not need to describe every
squirrel or machine part for a strong result.

## Feedback focus

Praise recognition of the transformation and the inventors’ action. Suggest one
specific machine or setting detail when the central idea is already present.
