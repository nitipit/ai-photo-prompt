# Challenge M1–M3–03: Moon Repair Shop

## Concept

In a floating workshop at night, three small animal mechanics repair a giant
crescent moon resting on a workbench. One uses an oversized wrench, one patches
a crack with golden material, and one inspects the work with a flashlight. Tools,
stars, and clouds surround the workshop.

The visual joke is an ordinary repair shop taking care of the moon.

## Core scoring anchors

- Giant crescent moon
- Animal mechanics repairing the moon
- Visible repair tools
- Floating workshop at night

## Optional details

- Golden patch over a crack
- Oversized wrench
- Flashlight
- Stars and clouds
- Distant city below

## Example short prompt

> ช่างซ่อมสัตว์กำลังซ่อมดวงจันทร์ในเวิร์กช็อปลอยฟ้า

## Evaluation notes

This challenge introduces an impossible job with a clear focal object. The moon,
repair action, mechanics, and floating night workshop matter more than every
tool or background element.

## Feedback focus

Praise recognition of the unusual job and the object being repaired. Suggest one
repair detail or atmosphere detail when the main concept is already clear.
