# Challenge M1–M3–04: Painted Ocean Door

## Concept

In a neon city alley, a small robot paints a door on a brick wall. When the
painting is finished, the painted door becomes real and opens into an underwater
world filled with coral, colorful fish, and blue light spilling onto the wet
street.

The visual joke is a painting that becomes a doorway to another world.

## Core scoring anchors

- Small robot painting a door on a wall
- Painted door becoming a real open doorway
- Underwater world behind the door
- Neon city alley

## Optional details

- Paintbrush and glowing paint
- Coral
- Colorful fish
- Blue light from the doorway
- Brick wall and wet street

## Example short prompt

> หุ่นยนต์วาดประตูบนกำแพง แล้วประตูเปิดไปสู่โลกใต้ทะเล

## Evaluation notes

This challenge rewards a clear transformation between two connected worlds. The
robot, painted doorway, underwater scene, and city alley are more important than
every fish or color detail.

## Feedback focus

Praise the player for describing the transformation and the relationship between
the two environments. Suggest one transition or atmosphere detail when the main
idea is already clear.
