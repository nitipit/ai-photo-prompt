# Challenge P4–P6–05: Flying Whale Amusement Park

## Concept

A friendly blue whale flies through an evening sky. On the whale’s back is a
small colorful amusement park with a Ferris wheel, a roller coaster, and bright
lights. Small animals ride the attractions while clouds drift around the whale.

The visual joke is a flying whale carrying an amusement park on its back.

## Core scoring anchors

- Whale flying in the sky
- Amusement park on the whale’s back
- Small animals riding the attractions
- Evening sky setting

## Optional details

- Ferris wheel
- Roller coaster
- Colorful lights
- Clouds
- Sunset colors

## Example short prompt

> วาฬบินอยู่บนท้องฟ้าและมีสวนสนุกอยู่บนหลัง มีสัตว์ตัวเล็กเล่นเครื่องเล่น

## Evaluation notes

This challenge is intended to add a larger, more cinematic scene to the
ป.4–ป.6 set. The whale, flying action, and amusement park relationship should
matter more than naming every ride or background detail.

## Feedback focus

Praise recognition of the unusual relationship between the whale and the
amusement park. Suggest one composition or atmosphere detail when the main
scene is already clear.
