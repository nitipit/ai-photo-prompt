# Challenge P4–P6–01: Dragon Goalkeeper

## Concept

A friendly purple dragon wearing a goalkeeper jersey dives through the air to
block a giant soccer ball in a castle courtyard. Small animal players watch and
cheer around a vine-covered goal, with a rainbow behind the castle.

The visual joke is a fantasy football match where a dragon is the goalkeeper.

## Core scoring anchors

- Purple dragon as goalkeeper
- Dragon actively diving or catching/blocking the ball
- Giant soccer ball
- Football field in a castle courtyard

## Optional details

- Small animal players
- Goal made from vines
- Goalkeeper jersey and gloves
- Rainbow
- Castle towers

## Example short prompt

> มังกรสีม่วงเป็นผู้รักษาประตู กำลังพุ่งรับลูกฟุตบอลยักษ์ในสนามหน้าปราสาท

## Evaluation notes

This level can expect clearer action, relationships, and setting than ป.1–ป.3.
The player still does not need to name every background animal or decoration.
The main action and fantasy setting should carry most of the evaluation.

## Feedback focus

Praise clear identification of the dragon’s role and action. Suggest adding the
castle setting or one visual detail when the main action is already present.
