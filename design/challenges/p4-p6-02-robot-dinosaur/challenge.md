# Challenge P4–P6–02: Robot Brushing a Dinosaur’s Teeth

## Concept

A small blue robot wearing round goggles stands on a ladder and uses an
oversized toothbrush to brush the teeth of a very large friendly green
dinosaur. The dinosaur smiles with its mouth open, holds a tiny mirror, and has
small puffs of toothpaste foam around its teeth.

The visual joke is a tiny robot doing an oversized job for a giant dinosaur.

## Core scoring anchors

- Small robot
- Robot brushing the teeth of a large dinosaur
- Oversized toothbrush
- Friendly dinosaur with its mouth open

## Optional details

- Ladder
- Round goggles
- Toothpaste foam
- Tiny mirror
- Bright dental room

## Example short prompt

> หุ่นยนต์ตัวเล็กกำลังแปรงฟันให้ไดโนเสาร์ยักษ์ด้วยแปรงสีฟันอันใหญ่

## Evaluation notes

This level can reward a clear subject relationship and action without requiring
every dental-room detail. A short prompt can still be strong when it makes the
robot, dinosaur, and oversized brushing action clear.

## Feedback focus

Praise the player for describing who is doing the action and who receives it.
Suggest one setting or prop detail when the main relationship is already clear.
